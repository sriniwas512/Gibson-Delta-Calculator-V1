Feature upgrades

  • Financing Module: Removed generic "CapEx" field. It now accurately calculates Daily Debt Service based on vessel price, equity, and loan dynamics.

  • Discounted NPV Delta: The heatmap now displays the Net Present Value (NPV) of the margin differences over the life of the TC, discounted at your chosen WACC.
